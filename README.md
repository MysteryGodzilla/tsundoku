# Tsundoku

*Tsundoku (積ん読): the Japanese word for buying books and letting them pile up unread.*

A simple, private catalogue of the books you own, so that when you're in a shop or browsing online
you can check in a few seconds whether you already have one.

- Add books by scanning the barcode, typing the ISBN, searching by title or author, or by hand.
- Search shows a clear **Already owned** label, even if you own the paperback and are looking at
  the Kindle edition.
- Works offline. Your library is yours: nobody else, not even whoever runs the server, can read it.
- Free and open source. No ads, no social features, no subscriptions.
